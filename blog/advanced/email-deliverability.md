# Advanced Email Deliverability Guide

## Understanding Email Deliverability

Email deliverability refers to the ability of an email to reach the recipient's inbox. It's influenced by various factors including sender reputation, email content, and infrastructure setup.

## Key Factors Affecting Deliverability

### 1. Sender Reputation
- IP reputation
- Domain reputation
- Engagement metrics

### 2. Authentication
- SPF (Sender Policy Framework)
- DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail)
- DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting & Conformance)

### 3. Content Quality
- Avoid spam trigger words
- Proper HTML structure
- Text-to-image ratio

## Technical Best Practices

### Infrastructure
- Use dedicated IPs for high-volume sending
- Implement proper bounce handling
- Set up feedback loops with major ISPs

### List Management
- Regular list cleaning
- Double opt-in process
- Easy unsubscribe mechanism

## Monitoring and Improvement

### Tools and Metrics
- Delivery rate
- Open and click rates
- Bounce rates
- Spam complaint rates

### A/B Testing
- Subject lines
- Send times
- Content variations

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

1. Purchased email lists
2. Ignoring bounce handling
3. Inconsistent sending patterns
4. Poor list hygiene

## Advanced Techniques

### Warm-up Process for New IPs
1. Start with small volumes
2. Gradually increase sending volume
3. Monitor engagement metrics

### Inbox Placement Testing
- Regular testing with seed lists
- Monitoring placement across providers
- Adjusting based on results
